What's different

Find each other, and choose.

Two things make everyday ride coordination worse than it should be: you can't find people going your way, and when an app does match you, it takes the choice away. Coast fixes both.

01 — Control

You choose who

See who's going your way, with their name and rating, and decide who to ask. Nobody is assigned to you.

Usually: a pooled ride puts you in a car with whoever the algorithm picked.
02 — Privacy

Your location stays fuzzy

Coast never stores where you actually are. Every point is nudged 60–180 m before it's saved — enough to match you, not enough to find your door.

Usually: your exact coordinates sit in someone's database forever.
03 — Money

No payments through the app

You settle the fare between yourselves — cash, UPI, whatever you agree. Coast doesn't process it and doesn't take a cut of your ride.

Usually: the platform sits in the middle of every rupee and skims it.

Safety

Built for getting in a car with someone you just met.

Trust is earned with real controls, not a checkmark. Here's what Coast actually gives you.

Match your own gender only

Optional, and it works both ways — you only see, and are only seen by, riders of the same gender.

Block or report anyone

From the rider card or the match screen. Repeat reports against the same account suspend it automatically.

Every ride is rated

Both people rate each other 1–5 after the ride. Ratings are real — there's no fake score to pad a profile.

Share your trip in one tap

Send who you're riding with, where, and when to someone you trust, straight from the app.
