How a ride comes together.

  1. Post your trip

    Say where you're headed, when you're leaving, how many seats you have and roughly what the cab costs.

  2. See who's near

    A live map shows people within 5 km going your way. Tap one to see their trip.

  3. Send a request

    They accept or decline. Once they accept, you chat in the app. Numbers stay private unless someone chooses to share theirs.

  4. Ride and rate

    Meet at the pin, split the fare, then rate each other from 1 to 5.

Controls you'll actually use.

Match your own gender only

Optional. When it's on, you only see riders of the same gender, and they only see you.

Block and report

On every profile and on the match screen. Repeat reports against the same account suspend it automatically.

Chat before you share a number

Once a request is accepted, sort out the pickup inside Coast without handing out your phone number.

Ratings that are real

Both people rate each other from 1 to 5 after the ride. There's no padded score.

Share your trip

Send who you're riding with, where and when to someone you trust, in one tap.

No money in the app

You settle the fare yourselves, with cash or UPI. Coast doesn't process it and doesn't take a cut.

Questions riders ask.

Is Coast free?

Yes. Installing and using Coast costs nothing, and Coast doesn't process or take a cut of your fare.

Is Coast a taxi service?

No. Coast doesn't employ drivers or dispatch cars. It helps people who are already making a trip find each other and share the cost.

Will I find someone going my way?

Coast works anywhere in India, but it can only match you with riders who are on it. It gets better with every person on your route, so bring a friend or a coworker who makes the same trip.

Who can use it?

Coast is for people aged 18 and over. Read the terms of service for the full rules.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Coast is on Android for now.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Profile tab in the app and delete your account there. Your profile, trips, ratings and history are removed right away. You can also follow the steps on the delete account page.

Riding the same route every day? Bring one person.

Coast gets better with every rider on your route. Install it, then send it to the person you'd share a ride with.
