The idea

You're not the only one heading that way.

Open Coast and it shows you people nearby going where you're going. One of you asks, the other says yes, and you split the ride.

Why Coast

You pick who. Not an algorithm.

Pooled rides assign you a stranger, sight unseen. On Coast you see them first — name, rating, route — and it’s your call.
