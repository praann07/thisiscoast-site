Yours stays yours.

You choose who you ride with, and what they get to know about you.

  • Your location is blurred

    Trip locations are shifted by 60 to 180 metres before they are saved.

  • Only while the app is open

    Coast uses your location in the foreground. It doesn’t track you in the background.

  • Your number stays private

    It’s hidden unless you switch sharing on in Profile. Until then, you talk in the app.

  • Talk inside the app

    Once a ride is accepted, you can message each other inside Coast.

  • Block or report anyone

    Use a person’s profile to block them or report them.

  • No payments in the app

    Coast never handles your money. You agree the fare between you.
