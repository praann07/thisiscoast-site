Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Short version

The Coast mobile app and the coastapp.in website do not use cookies, web beacons, pixels, local storage for tracking, device fingerprinting, or any similar technology to identify, profile, or track you.

What this means in practice

  • The mobile app is not a web browser and sets no cookies. It stores your sign-in session in an encrypted area of your device so you do not have to log in every time; that data never leaves your device and is not used for tracking.
  • The website is a set of static pages. It loads no third-party analytics, advertising, or social-media scripts. The only scripting on the site is a small animation that reveals sections as you scroll, and the waitlist form, which sends what you type directly to our own database. Neither reads or writes a cookie.

Third-party services

The app uses services such as Supabase, Google Firebase, Expo, and Esri map tiles to function (see the Privacy Policy for the full list and what data each receives). These are backend and infrastructure services accessed by the app over the network; they are not embedded browser trackers and do not place cookies on your device through Coast.

Changes

If we ever add website analytics or any technology that would use cookies or similar storage, we will update this page first and, where required, ask for your consent.
